# Reports and exports

[Back to overview](../README.md)

The product can assemble case reports and research memoranda from current case records and saved research, and provides PDF and DOCX export formats for relevant research outputs.

## Research memorandum

The export builder prepares a memorandum with the research question, jurisdictional context, substantive response and source warnings, as available. When state-law sources are search-derived, the export layer includes a warning that current statutory text requires independent verification.

## Case-level reports

Reports are generated from selected groups of documents, timeline events, issues, facts, and prior research. The evidence assembler distinguishes original documents from prior model output. A full case packet or multi-document review is a synthesis of bounded case material, not an assurance that every page or all relevant authority was examined.

## Reuse

Exports are intended for preparation, internal organization, and discussion with legal counsel; inspect each output for incomplete sections, wrong citations, and context truncation.
